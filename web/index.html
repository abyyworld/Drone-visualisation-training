What this does

Every uploaded image is first checked by a domain classifier. Turbine blades and solar panels are routed to the matching detector; anything else is refused rather than guessed at. Detections are scored into a severity band and rolled up into an inspection verdict you can export.

Wind turbine blades

Detects corrosion, cracks and surface peeling. There is no “healthy” class by design — an image with no detections is the healthy result.

Solar panels RGB only

Detects soiling, bird droppings, cracks, discoloration, vegetation and missing modules.

Cannot detect hotspots, cell defects, bypass-diode failure or offline modules. Those are electrical faults visible only in thermal infrared, and no visible-light model can find them — including this one.

Inference runs locally with ONNX Runtime Web. Images are processed on your device and are never transmitted. Results are a screening aid, not a certified structural assessment — confirm findings before acting on them.
