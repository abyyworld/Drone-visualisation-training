HOW A NUMBER IS JUDGED
  Every confirmed box is attributed to the person it sat on for the most frames, so
  finding another person and numbering the same person twice cannot be confused. A
  plain count cannot tell them apart and scores a detector that flickers higher.

    reached   of the people in view, how many ever got a number at all
    each      how many numbers those people got between them, per person
    stray     numbers issued to boxes that sat on nobody
    on person share of drawn boxes that were on a labelled person

WHAT THE COLUMNS SAY
  The first row is what was flying before any of this. The last is what ships. Thirteen
  more people in every hundred get a number, each of them gets slightly fewer numbers,
  and four boxes in five sit on somebody instead of three in four.

  Stray went the wrong way, 101 to 149, and that is the price. Looking at the whole
  frame every cycle is more chances to be confidently wrong as well as more chances to
  be right. Buying them back was measured: raising the confidence a new identity needs
  from 0.25 to 0.29 removes 30 of them and costs four points of people found, which is
  the wrong direction for a tool whose job is to not miss anyone.

WHAT THIS IS NOT
  Not the tablet. It is the tablet's cadence, geometry, model and tracker run on a
  desktop against labelled frames. Thermal throttling, the DSP's arithmetic and a real
  radio link are all absent.

  Not a crowd count. It is how well the thing numbers people it can see, and anyone
  under a tree, a canopy or a vehicle is not in either column.
